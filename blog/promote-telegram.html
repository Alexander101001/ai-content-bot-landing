How to Promote Your Telegram Bot (Free)

Method 1: Reddit

Best subreddits:

  • r/SideProject - Your bot is a side project
  • r/AItools - AI bots welcome
  • r/MachineLearning - Tech community
  • r/Telegram - Telegram users

Post value, not spam. Share your bot as a helpful tool.

Method 2: Hacker News

Show HN posts can bring 1000+ visitors.

Title: "Show HN: I built a free AI content generator Telegram bot"

Method 3: Product Hunt

Launch day can bring 500+ users.

Prepare: name, tagline, description, images.

Method 4: Twitter/X

Post threads about your bot:

  • How it works
  • What it does
  • Why it's useful
  • Link to try

Method 5: SEO

Create blog posts targeting:

  • "free AI tools"
  • "Telegram bots"
  • "AI content generator"
  • "free writing assistant"

Method 6: GitHub

Make your bot open source:

  • Developers will star it
  • Some will use it
  • Builds credibility

Method 7: Telegram Groups

Find relevant groups and share (don't spam):

  • AI groups
  • Tech groups
  • Business groups
  • Content creator groups
